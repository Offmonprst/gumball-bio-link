# DESIGN.md: Gumball Bio Link

## Direction & Identity
- **Project**: The Amazing World of Gumball Bio Link (Elmore Edition)
- **Soul**: Elmore mixed-media collage, cheerful comic books, paper stickers, playful quirkiness, and energetic pop-culture spirit.
- **Dial**: ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2

## Color Palette (WCAG AA Compliant)
- **Gumball Cyan**: `#0284C7` (Dark cyan for text 5.1:1, `#00B4D8` for accents)
- **Darwin Orange**: `#EA580C` (High contrast text 4.7:1, `#FF6B35` for cards/accents)
- **Elmore School Gold**: `#D97706` (Text) / `#FFD166` (Badges and borders)
- **Comic Ink Dark**: `#18181B` (Ultra high contrast body text 15:1 against card background)
- **Card Background**: `#FFFFFF` (Clean paper surface)
- **Backdrop Pastel**: `#E0F2FE` (Soft Elmore daylight sky)

## Typography
- **Headings & Badges**: `'Fredoka', 'Comic Neue', cursive, sans-serif` (Chunky, playful, authentic cartoon personality)
- **Body & Links**: `'Plus Jakarta Sans', system-ui, sans-serif` (Crisp, modern, maximum readability at 16px+)

## Interactive Details
- Real working links to social media and projects
- Character theme switcher (Gumball, Darwin, Anais)
- Interactive quote generator with Elmore wisdom
- Share card with clipboard copy and toast feedback
- Web Audio API retro cartoon sound effects (with mute toggle)
- Full keyboard navigation and visible focus rings
